      How this is calculated

      • Human milk is entered per kg per day. Fortifier and supplements are entered as the total amount per day, not per kg.
      • Each product's daily amount is multiplied by its composition. Results are divided by weight to give per kg per day.
      • Fluid includes human milk and the supplements measured in ml. The protein:energy ratio is grams of protein per 100 kcal.
      • ESPGHAN 2022 gives vitamin D per kg per day (400–700 IU/kg/day) with a ceiling of 1000 IU/day. ESPGHAN 2010 gave it per day (800–1000 IU/day).
      • Conversions: vitamin D 1 µg = 40 IU; vitamin A 1 IU = 0.3 µg retinol.
      • Status is judged on the rounded value shown on screen.

      References: Embleton ND, et al. Enteral nutrition in preterm infants (2022): a position paper from the ESPGHAN Committee on Nutrition. JPGN 2023;76:248–68; Agostoni C, et al. Enteral nutrient supply for preterm infants. JPGN 2010;50:85–91.
